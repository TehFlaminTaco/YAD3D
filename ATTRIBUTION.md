YAD3D is a derivative of **Space Station 13** as developed by the **/tg/station** community
(<https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation>). Everything that makes it look, sound and read like SS13 comes from
their work. The tree used is a 2020 snapshot of `tgstation` (commit `2d6c10c1ad31575e3214ba61b1f4d8abb7704fc4`, 2020-07-06).

## Deliberately left out

Some material in the /tg/station tree is under other terms. None of it is in this repository:

* **`goon/` icon directories** (art ported from Goonstation). Goonstation licenses its art CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial), which
  does not fit the rest of this project. The sprite builder skips them (`BAD_ICON_DIRS` in `tools/build_level.py`); an object that
  would have used one is simply left out.
* **Sounds that require individual attribution under CC BY 3.0** (the crate open/close sounds by lawnjelly, `cartoon_pop`
  by unfa, the Kevin MacLeod / Solus / other ambience and title music, the kMoon bullet-by sounds, shadoWisp's bat sounds).
  None is included.
* **Sounds cut from BBC recordings** (shotgun, revolver spin). Not included.
* Hit sounds by Taira Komori (`hit_punch`, `hit_kick`). Not included.

If you rebuild the assets from a different tree, check `sound/*/license.txt` and `sound/weapons/attributions.txt` in that tree
and extend the exclusions in `tools/build_sounds.py` first.
